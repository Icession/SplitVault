# SplitVault — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 20, 2026
**Last updated:** November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SplitVault mobile application ("the app,"
"we," "us") handles your information. Please read it carefully.

> **Plain-language summary:** SplitVault works entirely on your device. We do
> not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. There are no accounts,
> no ads, and no tracking.

## 1. Information we collect

**We do not collect any personal information.**

All data you enter into SplitVault — including your wallet balances,
transactions, savings goals, categories, and any profile details (such as your
name or email if you choose to enter them) — is stored **locally on your device
only**, using your device's local storage. This information never leaves your
device and is never sent to us or to any third party.

We do not operate servers that receive your data, and we have no ability to
access, view, or recover the information stored in the app.

## 2. How your information is used

Because your data stays on your device, it is used solely to provide the app's
features to you — for example, calculating balances, displaying your transaction
history, and tracking your savings goals.

## 3. Data sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your information to anyone, because we
do not have access to it.

## 4. Third-party services

SplitVault does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking
software. The app does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

## 5. Permissions

SplitVault does not request access to your contacts, location, camera,
microphone, photos, or other sensitive device features in order to function.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove your data
at any time by:

- Using the in-app options under **Settings → Data Management** (clear
  transactions or goals) or **Settings → Account → Reset App** (erase
  everything), or
- Uninstalling the app, which removes all locally stored data.

## 7. Children's privacy

SplitVault is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age
required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. Since the app collects no data, no personal
information from any user — including children — is gathered.

## 8. Security

Your information is stored within your device using the operating system's
standard app storage. Protecting the device itself (for example, with a screen
lock) is the most effective way to keep your in-app data private.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if future
versions of the app add features such as optional cloud backup or accounts. When
we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be
communicated within the app or its store listing.
